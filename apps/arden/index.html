Arden for Android

Set up Arden Hub

Arden is a local-first AI companion that runs on your own PC. Arden Hub shares it with the rest of the house, and the app is your hub, on your phone.

Arden is in early testing. The hub's setup guide arrives with Arden's first public release.

1. Run Arden Hub

Arden Hub runs on a PC at home (it can use that PC's graphics card), or in Docker. Open it in a browser and create the first person.

2. Give it an https address your phone trusts

The app only connects over https://: use your Tailscale name, or a home name such as https://ai.home behind a reverse proxy, with your home's root certificate installed on the phone (Settings → Security → Encryption & credentials → Install a certificate → CA certificate).

3. Connect the app

  1. Install Arden from Google Play and open it.
  2. Enter your hub's address, like https://ai.home, and tap Connect. The app checks it really is Arden Hub.
  3. Sign in as yourself.

If something's wrong

The app saysWhat to do
The certificate isn't trustedInstall your home's root certificate on the phone, or use your Tailscale address.
Couldn't find that addressCheck the spelling, and that the phone is on your home network or Tailscale.
It doesn't look like ArdenThe address reaches something else, such as a plain Ollama server: check the port.
