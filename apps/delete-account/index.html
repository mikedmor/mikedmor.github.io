Account and data deletion

Delete an account or your data

For the Android apps Muny, Hearth and Arden (by mikedmor). There's no account with me to delete: your account and data live on the server you run, so deleting them happens there.

Your account on your server

Muny

  • A household member or viewer: the owner removes them under Settings → Household & account. Their sign-in and sessions go; the household's shared records (accounts, transactions) stay, since they belong to the household.
  • Everything: the owner stops the Muny container and deletes its data folder (muny.db and its backups). That is all of Muny's data.

Hearth

  • A person: an admin removes them under Settings → People.
  • Everything: stop the Hearth container and delete its data folder.

Arden Hub

  • A person: the hub's owner removes their account from the hub's page. Their chats, records and keys are deleted with it. Anyone can sign their own devices out.
  • Everything: uninstall Arden Hub and delete its data folder.

If someone else runs the server you use, ask them: only they can delete data there.

Data on your phone

  1. Sign out in the app. In Muny this also deletes the offline copy.
  2. Turn notifications off in the app's notification settings. This deletes the phone's push token and keys, and stops your server sending to it.
  3. Uninstall the app, or Settings → Apps → (the app) → Storage → Clear storage. This removes everything the app kept on the phone.

Data held by me

None. The push relay that passes on notifications stores nothing and keeps no logs, so there's nothing to delete there. See the privacy policy.
